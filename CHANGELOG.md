# Changelog

## 1.0.0 – first release

- Monster levels: every monster gains a level every 3 Minecraft days (up to level 20), with more health and damage.
- Elite tiers: Strong, Elite and Legendary, with more health, damage and speed.
- 8 combat powers: Venomous, Blazing, Freezing, Hurling, Vampiric, Thorned, Blinking and Summoning.
- Elite names show their tier, powers and level.
- Colored particles around elites; Legendaries glow up close and don't burn in daylight.
- Elite loot tables: resources and enchanted gear, only when killed by a player.
- Safe zone around the world spawn (128 blocks).
- Commands: `/elite spawn`, `/elite info`, `/elite level`.
- Config file and datapack tags (blacklist / whitelist) for other mods' monsters.
